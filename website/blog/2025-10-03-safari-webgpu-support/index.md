With the release of Safari 26, WebGPU – and `strahl` – is now supported without any flags on Safari and Chrome.

<!-- truncate -->

It's been long in the making, and available in Technology Preview since [2023](https://webkit.org/blog/14879/webgpu-now-available-for-testing-in-safari-technology-preview/). With this release, WebGPU can be considered ready for production use on major platforms, including especially iOS devices.

Initially announced in [June 2025](https://webkit.org/blog/16993/news-from-wwdc25-web-technology-coming-this-fall-in-safari-26-beta/#webgpu), the release of iOS 26 and macOS 26 reached general availability mid-September 2025.

`strahl` has been tested on Safari 26, both on macOS and iOS, and works as expected.
